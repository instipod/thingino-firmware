Firewall

Accept new inbound connections only from trusted addresses

Status: unknown

One IPv4/IPv6 address or CIDR subnet per line.

When enabled, every service on the camera (web UI, SSH, RTSP, ONVIF, mDNS, ...) is reachable only from the trusted addresses.

Loopback, replies to connections the camera opens itself, and DHCP replies are always allowed. The firewall is not applied in captive-portal mode.

Saving is refused if the address you are connecting from is not trusted.
